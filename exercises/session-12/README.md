# Session 12: Capstone — AI Transformation Rollout Plan

## Objective
Pull together everything from Sessions 1-11 into a comprehensive, presentable AI transformation plan for your team or organization.

## Exercise: Build Your Rollout Plan (20 min)

Use the template below to create a transformation plan that you'll present to the group.

### Step 1: Current State Assessment (5 min)
Reference your Session 1 pain points and Session 11 baseline metrics.

- What are your team's top 3 development workflow pain points?
- What tools are you currently using?
- What's your current sprint velocity / cycle time?
- What's your bug escape rate?

### Step 2: Tool & Workflow Selection (5 min)
Based on Sessions 3-8 hands-on experience:

- Which AI tool(s) will your team adopt? (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, etc.)
- Which workflow commands/patterns had the highest impact in training?
- What customizations does your team need? (Reference Session 9)
- What governance policies will you implement? (Reference Session 10)

### Step 3: Phased Rollout (5 min)

**Phase 1 — Pilot (Weeks 1-2):**
- Who are your 2-3 early adopters?
- Which project will they use as the pilot?
- What metrics will you track?
- What's the go/no-go criteria for Phase 2?

**Phase 2 — Team Adoption (Weeks 3-6):**
- Training plan for remaining team members
- Pair programming sessions between early adopters and new users
- Weekly check-ins on adoption and issues
- Quality gate implementation

**Phase 3 — Optimization (Weeks 7-12):**
- Custom workflow development (Session 9 patterns)
- Security audit integration into CI/CD (Session 10)
- ROI measurement and reporting (Session 11)
- Cross-team knowledge sharing

### Step 4: Success Metrics (3 min)
From Session 11 ROI worksheet:

- Target time savings: ___% reduction in routine coding tasks
- Target quality improvement: ___% fewer bugs in production
- Target process improvement: ___% reduction in sprint planning overhead
- Developer satisfaction target: ___/5

### Step 5: Risk Mitigation (2 min)
- **Skill atrophy risk** → No-AI days, explain-before-commit policy (Session 2/5)
- **Over-reliance risk** → Quality gates, human review requirements (Session 6/10)
- **Security risk** → Security audit workflow, governance framework (Session 10)
- **Adoption resistance** → Start with pain points, show quick wins, don't force

## Presentation Format (10 min per team)

Each team/individual presents:
1. **The Before** (2 min) — Current pain points, baseline metrics
2. **The Plan** (5 min) — Tool selection, phased rollout, governance
3. **The Expected Impact** (2 min) — ROI projection, success metrics
4. **Q&A** (1 min) — Group feedback
